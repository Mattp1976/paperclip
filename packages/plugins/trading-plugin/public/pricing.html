Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period — no partial refunds, no surprise charges.

What happens if I downgrade?

Your extra hypotheses and features remain active until the current period ends. After that, excess hypotheses are paused (not deleted) and you can re-activate them if you upgrade again.

Is there a free trial for Pro or Elite?

The Free plan gives you a permanent taste of the platform. We don't currently offer time-limited trials, but you can upgrade and cancel within the first 7 days for a full refund.

Do you offer annual pricing?

Annual plans are coming soon with a 20% discount. Join the waitlist by emailing team@traider.ai.

Is my trading data secure?

Your API keys are encrypted at rest and in transit. We never store your exchange passwords. All connections use OAuth where available and encrypted credential storage otherwise.

Can I use trAIder for real trading?

The Elite plan includes live trading connectors for Alpaca (stocks), OANDA (forex), and IBKR (futures). Paper trading is available on Pro. All strategies start in simulation before going live.
